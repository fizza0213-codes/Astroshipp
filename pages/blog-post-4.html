Typography Example Post

Charles North — Sat Nov 05 2022

Typography is one of the most underrated tools in web design. Long before a visitor reads a single word, the type on your page is already shaping how trustworthy, modern, or polished your site feels.

Choosing a typeface

Stick to one or two font families at most — one for headings, one for body text, or a single versatile family for both. Too many typefaces on one page create visual noise and make the design feel unplanned. Pair a distinctive display font for headings with a highly readable font for paragraphs.

Size, spacing, and rhythm

Line height, letter spacing, and consistent scale between heading levels all contribute to readability. A good rule of thumb is a line height between 1.4 and 1.7 for body copy, and a clear size jump between each heading level so the hierarchy is obvious at a glance.

Contrast and color

Text needs enough contrast against its background to be comfortably readable, especially for longer paragraphs. Pure black on pure white can feel harsh for extended reading — a softer, slightly desaturated dark gray is often easier on the eyes while still staying clearly legible.
